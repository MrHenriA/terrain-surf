05

Why this mission

The challenge asks for a localized, integrated Mars view that joins multiple NASA missions to help humans plan and conduct science. This prototype joins MRO terrain with Perseverance observations in a crew excursion workflow. Optical and topographic data are included; slope is derived. Historical MEDA pressure and local mast air temperature add atmospheric context. Ionizing radiation is not loaded.
