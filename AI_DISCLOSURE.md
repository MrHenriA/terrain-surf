# AI / Super Intelligence assistance

**Human direction:** Henri originated the project concept, selected its goals and visual direction, supplied the existing rover, evaluated the interface and requested the Marswalk, terrain, route and weather workflows.

**OpenAI Codex assistance:** research and source comparison; code generation and debugging; data conversion and provenance tooling; tutorial implementation; automated browser tests; documentation, submission-draft wording, capture automation and video editing instructions. Human direction and review shaped these outputs. Development conversations also used Gemini suggestions about browser graphics and tutorial approaches.

**Scientific data:** NASA/USGS measurements and the cited regional dust product were downloaded from the credited sources and processed into browser subsets. AI assisted the code used to process and interpret them. It did not invent the archived measurements. Their source identities, transformations, gaps and limitations are preserved in the data manifests.

**Images and video:** the rover visual is a pre-existing BlenderKit asset; scientific images come from their credited archives. The demo is a real recording of this app, with waits/travel compressed and captions added through an AI-assisted editing workflow. It is not a generatively synthesized Mars mission. No AI-generated audio or music is included. The PDF and app visuals illustrate a virtual visit and do not establish actual rover performance.
