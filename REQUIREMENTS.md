## Alignment with the selected challenge

The astronaut's task is a local science excursion, supported by a virtual rover scout. The app integrates MRO terrain/imagery, Perseverance observations and atmospheric context to connect routes, destination evidence and planning assumptions. Users can plan stops, inspect elevation and slope tradeoffs, record observations and export a sourced report.

The challenge suggests at least three data types; the app includes optical, topographic and atmospheric material. Slope is a derivative of topography, not a separate mission dataset. Weather and radiation are example considerations rather than universal mandatory layers. The app does not include radiation dose or calibrated wheel/soil/energy estimates, and does not represent historical dust maps as current local weather or forecasts.

Final eligibility, judging and rule interpretation remain with the organizers. This mapping describes implemented project features and submission materials, not an award or safety certification.
